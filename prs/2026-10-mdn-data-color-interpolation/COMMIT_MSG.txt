feat(css): add color-interpolation property

Add `color-interpolation` to css/properties.json. The property is
defined in SVG 2 and supported in all major browsers
(css.properties.color-interpolation in BCD), and MDN already documents
it. Only this data was missing it; its sibling
`color-interpolation-filters` is already here.

Values follow the spec:
https://w3c.github.io/svgwg/svg2-draft/painting.html#ColorInterpolationProperty

Adds the l10n key `limitedSVGElementsContainerGraphicsGradientUseAnimate`
for "applies to" and registers it in properties.schema.json.
